How we handle your information

This is placeholder content only. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch. We are collecting personal information needed to process orders, deliver goods, and support payments.

The site may store your name, phone number, delivery address, account login details, order history, and payment proof images. Data is stored in Supabase using the project’s Postgres database and Storage features.

Payment receipts are stored securely in the payment-receipts storage bucket and associated with your order and user ID. We use this data to verify payments, process orders, and improve service quality.
